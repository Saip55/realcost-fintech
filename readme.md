# ⚡ RealCost — Money is Time. See What Things Actually Cost.

> **Transform everyday spending into exact working hours traded and long-term compounding wealth.**

---

## 🌟 Vision & The Mindset Shift

Most budgeting tools only show you where your money *went*. **RealCost** shows you **what you traded to get it**, and what that money could become if invested in your future instead.

Every single purchase carries two hidden metrics:

1. ⏱️ **Life Energy Traded**: How many hours and minutes of your finite work life were required to earn that cash?
2. 📈 **Opportunity Cost**: What would that exact amount grow into over 10, 20, or 30 years if invested in a simple index fund?

---

## ✨ Key Experience & Features

### 💳 3D RealCost Baseline Card
An interactive, glassmorphic card widget that visualizes your personal hourly wage baseline, 20-year single-hour compound projection value, and customized time-to-money parameters.

### ⏱️ Life-Hour Ledger & Smart Categorization
Log any transaction (e.g., ₹3,000 gadget or ₹150 coffee) and instantly calculate:
- **Work Hours Traded**: Exact hours and percentage of an 8-hour workday shift required.
- **Future Compound Value**: Projected wealth over your chosen time horizon.
- **Smart Auto-Categorization**: 50+ keywords automatically tag expenses into Food, Subscriptions, Transport, Shopping, and Health.

### 💥 Subscription Leak & Audit Engine
Unmask silent recurring monthly drains. Interactively test marking subscriptions for cancellation to uncover your **Recoverable Lifetime Wealth**.

### 🔮 What-If Financial Freedom Simulator
Experiment with minor daily or monthly habit cuts (like saving ₹100/day on impulse treats) to see a live visual compounding trajectory over 5, 10, 20, or 30 years.

### 🌐 Interactive 3D WebGL Globe & About Us Page
Explore the mathematical architecture behind RealCost with an interactive 3D WebGL globe, interactive side-by-side expense comparisons, and transparent equations.

## 🚀 Quick Start (Run Locally)

Experience RealCost on your local machine in 3 quick steps:

```bash
# 1. Clone the repository
git clone https://github.com/Saip55/realcost-fintech.git
cd realcost-fintech

# 2. Install dependencies
npm install

# 3. Start local development server
npm run dev
```

Open **[http://localhost:8080/](http://localhost:8080/)** in your web browser to test all live features!
